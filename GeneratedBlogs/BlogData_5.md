
[K[?25hSure! Below is a basic example of what you can include in your blog post YAML front matter for a publish-ready blog post:

```yaml
---
title: "Title of the Blog Post"
date: "YYYY-MM-DD"
categories:
  - Category 1
  - Category 2
tags:
  - Tag 1
  - Tag 2
description: "Brief description of the blog post content."
keywords: "keyword1, keyword2, keyword3"
author:
  name: Author Name
  email: author@example.com
canonical_url: http://example.com/post-title
image:
  src: /path/to/image.jpg
  alt: Alt text for the image
cta:
  type: affiliate
  link: "https://affiliate-link.com"
---
```

### SEO Metadata

- **Title**: The title of your blog post.
- **Date**: The date the post was published, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Categories**: A list of tags or categories for your post.
- **Tags**: Additional tags that can help categorize your post.
- **Description**: A brief summary of what the post covers.
- **Keywords**: Relevant keywords to improve search engine visibility.
- **Author**: Information about the author, including their name and email.
- **Canonical URL**: The canonical URL for your post.

### Affiliate CTA

- **Type**: Specify the type of call-to-action (e.g., affiliate).
- **Link**: The link to the affiliate product or offer you want to promote.

This YAML front matter can be used in various blogging platforms and content management systems. Make sure to tailor it to your specific requirements and platform's format.
